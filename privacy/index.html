Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Kinboard (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a shared family dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the basic account information needed to create and secure your Kinboard account, such as your name, email address, and profile image. If you connect Google Calendar, we access the calendar data and calendar list information needed for the calendar features you choose to use.

How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve Kinboard; authenticate your account; sync features you request; and communicate about the service. We do not sell your personal information or use Google data for advertising.

Google user data

Kinboard requests the Google Calendar calendar.readonly scope to list calendars in your Google account and read event titles, dates, and times from calendars you enable, so Kinboard can display those events on your household dashboard. Calendar data is requested for the date range being displayed and is used to provide this feature.

If Kinboard requests the calendar.events scope, it is used to create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events when you ask Kinboard to make those changes. Kinboard does not use this permission to make changes without your request. Calendar items you add using KinBoard features may instead be stored in KinBoard and displayed on your dashboard; they are not written to Google Calendar unless the feature specifically says so.

Google Calendar data is processed only to provide or improve user-facing calendar features, is not used for advertising, and is not sold or transferred to others except as necessary to provide the service, comply with law, or as you direct. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We request only the permissions needed for the features you choose.

Sharing and retention

We do not share personal information except with service providers that help us operate Kinboard, when required by law, or with your direction. We retain information while your account is active and delete it when you request account deletion, subject to limited legal or security retention.

Your choices

You may disconnect Google access through your Google Account settings or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@kinboard.app.
