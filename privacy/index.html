Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Kinboard (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a shared family dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and profile image, along with authentication tokens needed to keep your account connected. If you connect Google Calendar, we access your calendar list and the event titles, dates, and times from calendars you enable. We request event information for the date range shown in the dashboard. If you separately connect event access, we also request permission to create events in Google Calendar.

How we use Google user data

Kinboard uses the Google Calendar calendar.readonly scope to find calendars available in your Google account and read events from the calendars you enable. We use calendar names and event information to let you choose calendars, display their events in your household dashboard, and keep that view current. We store your selected calendar IDs and display preferences, and store Google OAuth access and refresh tokens so the calendar feature can access Google Calendar on your behalf. Event details are retrieved from Google Calendar for display and are not used for advertising, sold, or used to build profiles.

Kinboard requests the Google Calendar calendar.events scope only when you choose to connect event access. This additional permission lets Kinboard create events in a Google calendar you select. When you submit an event and choose one of your connected Google calendars as its destination, Kinboard sends the event title, start and end times, and all-day setting to Google Calendar to create that event. If you choose “Kinboard only,” the item is stored in Kinboard and no event is created in Google Calendar. Kinboard currently uses this permission to create events; it does not use it to edit or delete Google Calendar events. You can decline this additional permission and continue using Kinboard's calendar features that do not require it, or revoke access later in your Google Account settings.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features that you choose to use. We do not use it for targeted or personalized advertising, sell it, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Kinboard does not sell or disclose Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the service, when required by law, or when you direct us to do so. We use infrastructure and service providers to host and operate Kinboard; those providers may process data, including Google user data, only to provide services to Kinboard and are required to protect it. We do not otherwise transfer Google user data to third parties.

Security

Kinboard’s hosted service runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores account records and Google OAuth tokens in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare states that D1 data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and that transfers between Workers and D1 use TLS. Kinboard communicates with Google APIs over HTTPS, and sends Google access tokens in server-to-server authorization headers to make the calendar requests you ask for. Tokens are stored in the server-side database and are not made available to dashboard pages as browser data. Access to the database is through the Kinboard Worker’s server-side binding; application credentials such as the Google client secret are configured as Cloudflare Worker secrets. Kinboard does not add a separate application-level encryption layer to OAuth tokens before storing them in D1. We limit use of these data and credentials to operating Kinboard and protecting connected accounts. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information and Google OAuth tokens while your Kinboard account is active and the Google connection remains in place. Calendar IDs and preferences are retained to remember your setup. Event details are requested from Google Calendar for display rather than kept as a separate event archive by Kinboard. You can revoke Kinboard’s Google access in your Google Account settings at any time. To request deletion of your Kinboard account and associated data, email privacy@kinboard.app. We will delete or de-identify associated data, including stored tokens, subject to information we must retain for legal or security reasons.

Your choices

You may disconnect Google access through your Google Account settings or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@kinboard.app.
